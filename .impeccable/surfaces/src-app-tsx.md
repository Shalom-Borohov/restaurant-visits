## Scope

The whole app shell and its screens: מסעדות (restaurants list), restaurant page, visit and add-dish sheet, ביקורים, הגדרות. Mode: Operate. Platform: iOS Home Screen web app, Ionic iOS mode, Hebrew RTL.

## Audience and job

The owner, one-handed at a restaurant table: log a dish camera-first; later rate it; on a revisit read what to order and what to skip at a glance.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each dish is a glazed plate: a round photo in a rim whose glaze colour carries the verdict, with the verdict word lettered beneath it. Refuses the category default of square thumbnails in a grey inset list.

OWN-WORLD: Tin-white glaze ground #fbfaf6, tiles #fffefa at 24px radius with soft cobalt-tinted shadows. Cobalt #1f3f8f owns the shell (a rounded header field, the primary action, חובה להזמין); turquoise #2a9d9a טעים; ochre #d39b2a בסדר; faded clay #a07a68 לא משהו; manganese brown #6b3b2a ink and לא להזמין, struck through. The header is plain cobalt with no ornament (the painted band was removed by the owner on 2026-11-11, spec 62). Suez One for large titles only; Rubik everywhere else.

STORY: The owner sees dishes waiting for a verdict, then their restaurants, each led by the plate of its best dish and showing its worst, and the camera plate in thumb reach.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cobalt header field with large title and search; the unrated card with two bisque-rimmed plates; restaurant tiles with a 58px rimmed plate and its verdict pill on the leading side; the "ביקור חדש" camera plate floating above a floating rounded tab bar.

FORM: Glazed plates (Armenian ceramics of Jerusalem), position 2 on the ordered list. seed:bce0ed054c4f8bf9

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
